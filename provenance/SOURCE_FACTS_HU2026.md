# Source-constrained evidence register: Hu et al. (2026)

Reference: Ruixin Hu, Weibing Xu, Jin Wang, Xiuli Du, Xiaomin Huang, Qingchuang Guo, Keming Pan, Dingqing Feng, Cheng Wang. “Study on the mechanical properties of an inerter friction damper.” Soil Dynamics and Earthquake Engineering 211 (2026) 110650. DOI: 10.1016/j.soildyn.2026.110650.

## Verified from publisher-indexed information
- Device topology: a ball-screw inerter connected in series with a friction energy-dissipation unit.
- A full-scale IFD specimen was fabricated and dynamically tested.
- The measured damping force is strongly nonlinear and contains high-order harmonic components.
- At zero preload, representative hysteresis regimes include 0.1, 0.5, and 1.0 Hz.
- The paper proposes a machine-learning method for the nonlinear device behavior.
- The paper defines an energy absorption-dissipation ratio Rp.
